I work on zero-knowledge proof systems in Rust, mostly GKR and sumcheck, zkVMs and recursion.

From 2023 to 2026 I was a senior zero-knowledge engineer at Scroll, on its zk-rollup proving stack. I started on the Halo2 zkEVM circuits, including EIP-4844 blobs, zstd compression and EIP-1559, and took them through Trail of Bits audits. I then moved to recursion: first Spartan-based, then a GKR recursion verifier for Ceno, Scroll's RISC-V zkVM. The verifier runs as a program on OpenVM's native VM and aggregates proofs in a binary tree up to a Halo2-wrapped root. After Scroll I built [Apogee VM](https://apogee.gweb3networks.com/docs), a RISC-V zkVM with a pure GKR prover and verifier; v1.0.0 came out in October 2026.

Before Scroll I worked in private equity: as an analyst at Alpine Capital Advisors in 2019, and in 2022 as a PE web3 analyst at NFTA, an early step into web3 where I ran an on-premises Ethereum node, archived on-chain trading data and began prototyping zero-knowledge proofs.
